The skill documents: the word is `kindcluster` (not `kind`); the engine is
charly's own engine word mapped 1:1 to `KIND_EXPERIMENTAL_PROVIDER`

preconditions; authoring a `kindcluster:` template + (optional-workload) deploy;
probing with `kube:` (nodes / wait-nodes / storageclass / raw) and why
`kube: addons` is k3s-only (kind ships kindnet, no Traefik/ServiceLB); the
engine-agnostic `kind load image-archive` path (`load docker-image` is broken on
podman); and `kind delete cluster` teardown.

merged home of `deploy:kindcluster`) rather than the new `layer-kind` repo, whose
